Conference Overview

The SWEeyam 2026 Developer Conference is designed for engineers who value depth over breadth. This is not a vendor showcase or marketing event — it is a technical gathering where industry leaders, alumni, and academic experts share production engineering insights.

Every session is curated for technical substance, practical applicability, and long-term value.
