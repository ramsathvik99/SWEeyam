Hackathon Overview

The SWEeyam 2026 Hackathon is a 36-hour intensive engineering challenge where teams build production-grade solutions to real industry problems.

This is not a race to build the fastest prototype — it is an opportunity to demonstrate engineering excellence through code quality, system design, security practices, and long-term maintainability.
